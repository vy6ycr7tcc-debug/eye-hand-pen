# "Eye, Hand, Pen": The Living Manuscript
## Philosophical Exegesis, Archetypal Mechanics, and Architectural Design

> *"The eye, as it is. It only sees what it sees.*  
> *And the hand, that must reach.*  
> *Where the reach meets the world, something itches.*  
> *What the reaction leaves behind: the mark.*  
> *The heart orchestrates. The pen writes. This is you.*  
> *And the pen is remade by what it chose to write.*  
> *The writing becomes the world."*

---

## 1. What This Project Is

**"Eye, Hand, Pen"** is a JavaScript cinematic animation (~4–5 minutes, auto-playing in the browser) that teaches the seven archetypal functions — Matrix, Potentiator, Catalyst, Experience, Significator, Transformation, Great Way — **entirely through one person's personal visual metaphors**, not through tarot cards or esoteric diagrams. 

It is an *explainer element*, not a game: the viewer watches, absorbs, rewatches. It will eventually live inside a larger contemplative game as one solid explanatory piece.

## 4. The Underlying Archetypal Mechanics (Source Ontology)

To ensure the film's visual metaphors remain pure to their metaphysical foundations:

1. **Catalyst is Begotten, Not Met**:
   - Catalyst is the offspring of the Matrix (unconscious unmoving mind) and Potentiator (yearning reach of the unconscious) uniting across the veil.
   - Never depict the Catalyst as an external entity "meeting" the Potentiator in space; it blooms *upon the palm* as the organic consequence of reaching.
2. **Upward Routing is Law**:
   - Nothing flows directly from the deep mind to the body or spirit. All archetypal energy ascends through the Veil into the conscious mind first, then branches outward.
   - In any architectural or visual expansion, never draw a direct deep-mind $\rightarrow$ body shortcut.
3. **The Body Octave is a Mirror-Inversion of Mind**:
   - If ever extended to the Body or Spirit octaves:
     - The Mind Matrix is **utter stillness** (the Eye).
     - The Body Matrix is **unceasing motion** (never still).
     - The Mind Potentiator **activates** (the Reach).
     - The Body Potentiator **regulates** rather than activates.
4. **Service-to-Self (STS) Stays Out of Focus**:
   - The film follows the light.
   - The left-hand path is acknowledged briefly as an unchosen branch at the Fork, but is never dwelt upon, never made attractive, and never made playable.
5. **Naming Conventions**:
   - Egyptian ordering is preserved for traditional titles; deck conventions are never mixed.

## 5. The Visual System: Albrecht Dürer Burin Engraving

The visual language is locked into an authentic **living illuminated Renaissance manuscript**:
- **Technique**: Albrecht Dürer and Leonardo da Vinci copperplate burin engraving. Cross-hatched burin shading, anatomical weight, burin feathering, plate tone. Procedural flat vectors are strictly forbidden for focal archetypes.
- **Palette**: Deep indigo/near-black `#050b16`, gold ink `#e8b64c` / `#ffe9a8`, warm off-white text `#f0e8d2`.
- **Typography**: Elegant serif intertitles (Georgia/Cormorant fallback), letter-spaced, centered, slow fades.
- **Pacing**: ~4–5 minutes total, ~30–40s per beat, 2–3s cross-dissolves, never hard cuts.
- **Text Rules**: Affirmative phrasing only (never "not X, not Y"); never address the viewer as "friends"; never name channeled entities, sessions, archives, or dates.
- **Images First, Words Second**: Every intertitle *follows* its visual beat — the viewer feels the concept before it is named.

## 6. Technical Architecture

- **Single Self-Contained Deliverable**:
  - `pieces/eye-hand-pen/index.html` (1.2 MB, inline CSS + JS, base64-encoded WebP raster assets).
  - No build step, no npm, runs offline directly from `file://` or any static server without CORS restrictions.
- **Universal Deterministic Timeline**:
  - Pure function of elapsed time `t` driven by `requestAnimationFrame`.
  - Scrubbing and headless evaluation fully accessible via `window.__frame(time)`.
- **Responsive Geometry**:
  - Dynamic scaling `S = Math.min(width, height) / 900` with portrait scaling (`portraitScale = 0.72..0.78`) ensuring flawless rendering on mobile (390×844) and desktop (1920×1080).
- **Verification Guarantee**:
  - Verified via Playwright (`scratch/capture_stills.js`) asserting **0 console errors** across all beats on desktop and mobile.
